# 0018. Make Now mean in flight, sweep it at both ends, and cap it

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-13

## Context

STATE.md's Now section accreted into a changelog. In this very repository it
held a dozen entries, nearly all narrations of finished work, each dated,
each passing the audit, because the 90-day horizon bounds abandonment rather
than accumulation and the 150-line budget bounds documents rather than
status. A derived project showed the same disease at larger scale. The
rulebook predicted the failure in its own species section, a history file is
record content forced into a living file, and Now caught it anyway, because
"Now" was ambiguous between what is in flight and what is true of the
project, and every landing narration entered through the second reading.
Finishing work produces an itch to prove it happened; the commit already is
that proof.

## Options considered

- Allow the status reading and let Now describe the current project. The
  tree, the git log, and the decision records already carry all of it, and
  the living-document rules already ban inventory another source can answer.
- Give landings a home of their own, a changelog. The rulebook already
  answers this: chronology is never documented, and a changelog exists only
  where consumers upgrade through releases.
- Sharpen the definition but add no machine bound. The definition alone was
  effectively the old law, and it accreted anyway; a rule nobody is forced
  to meet decays into a preference.
- Cap Now at three, or ten. Three refuses legitimate parallel threads in a
  busy week; ten never fires. Five forces the cleaning conversation at the
  moment of accretion while leaving room for real work.

## Decision

An entry earns Now only while its work is genuinely unfinished, or while it
names a condition future work must honor that no other document carries.
Completing work deletes its entry in the same change; a narration of the
landing never replaces it. STATE.md is swept at both ends of every change,
before starting anything new by deleting finished or unconfirmable entries,
and after finishing for entries the change completed or invalidated. The
docs audit caps Now at five entries. The rulebook's STATE schema, the agent
guide's hard rule, and the audit all carry the same law.

## Consequences

STATE.md stays small enough to read in one breath, and its claims stay
verifiable. The first sweep under the new law shrank this repository's own
Now to its genuine content, deleting every landing narration including the
one this change's sibling decision had just added, which is the law applying
to its own authors. The opening sweep gives cleanup a scheduled owner, the
next agent to arrive, instead of hoping each author remembers. The delivery
gate needs no new item, because its intent-split placement item now catches
a landing narration as record content in a living file. Derived projects
adopt the definition, the sweeps, and the cap through the normal alignment
wave, which will shrink their STATE files sharply on first contact.
