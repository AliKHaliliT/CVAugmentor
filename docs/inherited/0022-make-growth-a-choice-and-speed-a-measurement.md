# 0022. Make growth a choice and speed a measurement

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-18

## Context

The treasury's second study catalogued the discipline's optimization
vocabulary, and most of what it holds cannot become style law. A technique
whose entry names a price wins in one context and loses in the next, and
mandating a conditional winner unconditionally is the first study's
definition of an anti-pattern. What survives as general is the discipline's
own core, and it splits on one fact. A measurement sees the code running on
today's data, so it cannot catch a growth-rate mistake at write time, when
the demo input is small and every loop is instant; the slowness arrives with
real data, after the code has frozen. Growth rate is also the one
performance property readable from the code alone, free of any measurement.
So the rule against optimizing early cannot govern growth, and the rule
demanding measurement cannot be satisfied by it. The study catalogs the two
schools, constant attention and the optimization stage approach, as opposed
entries; the ruling below is the treaty between them.

## Options considered

- Technique mandates in the conventions, a caching or batching or layout
  rule. Rejected: a style is a table of unconditional rulings, and every
  such technique is conditional by its own price clause.
- One merged gate item. Rejected: the two failures point in opposite
  directions and fire at different times, one while writing and one while
  optimizing, and a compound item cannot be weighed in one look.
- Continuous benchmarking with a regression ratchet. Rejected for a
  template: the study itself prices it at dedicated stable hardware and
  noise-stalled delivery, speculative scaffolding for demo workloads.
- Complexity annotations on every export's documentation. Rejected: burden
  without a reader, and the point-of-use truth item already holds any claim
  actually made.

## Decision

Two gate items, held byte-identical across the family. Growth honesty: what
each loop's or query's cost grows with is a choice, not an accident, and no
change buys a worse growth rate where a construction of equal effort exists.
The measured line: nothing is made faster without a measurement that
demanded it, and every optimization that lands records its measurement and
its price. The checkable half follows the immediate-return precedent at zero
new dependency. The Python styles select PERF and C4 in ruff, which catch
the mechanical growth accidents, and the client style enables slow-regex
from the Sonar plugin it already carries, catastrophic backtracking being a
growth accident inside a regular expression. The technique families stay in
the treasury as design-time reading, reached by pointer and never copied.

## Consequences

The gate grows to eighteen items and stays one text under the family
checker. Constant attention governs growth and the staged approach governs
everything priced, with neither maxim softening the other. A demo that is
fast enough stays untouched, because both items demand honesty rather than
speed. Each linter was proven against a planted defect before its first
clean run. Derived projects adopt the items and the lint through the normal
alignment wave.
