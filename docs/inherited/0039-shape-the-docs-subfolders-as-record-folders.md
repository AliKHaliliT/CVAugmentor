# 0039. Shape the docs subfolders as record folders

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-04

## Context

After the maintainer's reply, the adopting doctoral repository's docs zone
held the spine beside five subfolders: briefings and progress reports,
which are dated documents, and guides, technical notes, and research
notes, which are living documents. The rulebook sanctioned no subfolder
beyond the record folders and, in the host, the arrow manifests, and the
audit's allowance for any registered folder let living documents hide in
one, outside the naming and budget rules that see only flat files at the
top of docs/. The species sentence calling every dated document a record
was also ahead of the check, which held decisions and claims alone.

## Decision

A subfolder under docs/ is a record folder, nothing else, or in the host
the arrow manifests. Living organic documents are flat UPPERCASE files at
the top of docs/, one subject each, budgeted and named, because that is
the only shape the rules can see. A record folder beyond decisions and
claims holds dated documents named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-kebab-title.md`,
ordered by when they happened rather than by number, registered by its
own row in the index, and immutable like every record. The audits fail
any file below a subfolder that is not such a record in such a folder,
and the immutability check reads every record folder rather than two.

## Consequences

Briefings and progress reports keep their folders as dated records and
stop being edited. Guides, technical notes, and research notes fold into
flat documents or, where a note is really dated, into a record folder.
An adopting agent gets from the gate the answer the maintainer gave by
hand.
